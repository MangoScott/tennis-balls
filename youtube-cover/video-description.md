## Description

Two USTA 4.5 players. Both banged up, both running on empty. What could go wrong?

Fair warning before you hit play: this is not our best tennis. Not even close. We
both came into this match already hurting, and neither of us had the legs to do
anything about it. So instead of clean 4.5 ball-striking, you get short swings,
lazy footwork, unforced errors in bunches, and two guys visibly negotiating with
their own bodies between points.

We're posting it anyway, because sloppy matches are most of the tennis anyone
actually plays. Nobody films the day they're fresh and it's 72 degrees. This is
what a real 4.5 match looks like when you're tired, tight, and stubborn enough to
finish it.

What you'll see:
- Rally lengths that end early — for all the wrong reasons
- Second serves that shouldn't be second serves
- Points won on effort instead of skill
- Both of us hunting for the shortest possible way to end a rally
- The occasional accidentally good shot that keeps us coming back

If you're a 4.5 player who's ever gone out on a bad ankle, a cranky shoulder, or
four hours of sleep, you'll recognize a lot of this. Drop a comment with the
worst you've ever felt in a match you still refused to default.

Score, level, and honest self-assessment in the video. Be nice to us.
