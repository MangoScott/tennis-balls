# Wilson US Open Extra Duty – Tennis Ball Review (Weight, Bounce, Hit Test + Cut Open)

Most tennis balls suck, so I'm reviewing all of them one by one. First up: the Wilson US Open Extra Duty, my favorite ball of all time.

Every ball in this series gets the same four tests: weight on a kitchen scale, bounce height against a yardstick, a hit test on court, and a post-play bounce test to see how much it drops off. Then I cut it open to look at the rubber.

RESULTS
• Weight: ~59 g per ball, very consistent across the can
• Bounce (new): 25 in / 63.5 cm
• Bounce (after play): 25 in / 63.5 cm, no measurable drop
• Hit test: consistent, good bounce, doesn't fluff up much, easily two sessions per can
• Cut open: noticeably higher-quality rubber than other balls I've cut, and better than last year's US Open ball
