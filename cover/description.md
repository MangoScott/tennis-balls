the whole pitch.

## Notes

- Chapters only switch on if the first one is `0:00` and there are at least
  three of them, each ten seconds or longer.
- YouTube surfaces the first three hashtags above the title, so keep it to
  three and put the broadest one first.
- If the ball tier list is published somewhere, a line like
  `Our tennis ball rankings: [url]` sits well just above the hashtags.
